        ### Impact

        My research is cited in leading journals outside of economics, including in the cognitive sciences (e.g., *American Psychologist*, and *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*), the life sciences (e.g., *British Medical Journal* and *Molecular Biology and Evolution*), political science (e.g., *American Journal of Political Science*, *American Political Science Review*, and *Annual Review of Political Science*), and interdisciplinary areas (e.g., *Nature Human Behaviour*, *PNAS*, and *WIREs Climate Change*).

        It has been cited in popular books, including [*Origins*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1541617908) by Lewis Dartnell, [*The Journey of Humanity*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0593185994) by Oded Galor, [*The Weirdest People in the World*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0374173222) by Joseph Henrich, [*The Plague Cycle*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1982165332) by Charles Kenny, [*The Open Sea*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0691151741) by Joseph G. Manning, [*Grand Transitions*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0190060662) by Vaclav Smil, and [*Amber Waves*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/022655371X) by Catherine Zabinski.

        And it has been referenced in significant institutional reports and media outlets. This includes mentions in IMF's *World Economic Outlook*, World Bank's *Doing Business Report*, and platforms like the *World Economic Forum*, *Marginal Revolution*, and BBC’s *50 Things that Made the Modern Economy*. Additionally, my work has been featured in the *Wall Street Journal*, *Handelszeitung*, *Neue Zürcher Zeitung*, *Super Interessante Magazine*, *World Commerce Review*, and major Danish newspapers. I have also given testimony before the Danish Parliament’s European Affairs Committee.
